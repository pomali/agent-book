# Canon tests: The Unploughed Strip

Answer from canon only. Check yourself before the first session.

- Q: How many notches are on the tally after chapter 01? After chapter 06?
  A: Fifty-nine. Still fifty-nine. (D1, seed 06)
- Q: How wide and how long is the Share?
  A: Six paces by two hundred. (P6)
- Q: Which field is fallow in the summer of 1300, and what is sown on it at St Michael?
  A: Under-the-Alders; winter rye. (P5)
- Q: Baba and Katka are both at the pool. Can they both see the man with the wet hem?
  A: No. Nothing unseen shows itself to more than one person at a time. (U3)
- Q: The reader, in chapter 03, asks why the family keeps the Share.
  A: Not told yet. The reason is revealed in chapter 08. Katka does not know either. (D3, D4, BOOK rule 1)
- Q: Can Father Hermann drive the thing out of the pool with a blessing?
  A: No. The Church is neither their enemy nor their master. (U7)
- Q: What do the unseen do to the family after the Share is ploughed?
  A: Nothing violent. They withdraw: the house snake, the milk, the bees, the water. (U6, A2)
- Q: What does the family eat with its porridge: potatoes or turnips?
  A: Turnips. Potatoes are not in this world. (H4, H5)
- Q: Does the family own a spinning wheel? A clock? A chimney?
  A: None of them. Distaff and spindle; sun and bell; smoke through the roof. (H4, P9)
- Q: Why does Jakub plough the Share?
  A: To keep it: under Kunrat's charter unploughed land is waste and can be given to settlers. (A1, V2)
- Q: Is Kunrat cheating the village?
  A: No. He is honest and within his charter. Nobody is a villain. (V1, BOOK rule 3)
- Q: What does Katka ask at the pool in chapter 11, and what is the whole answer?
  A: "What is owed?" "A strip." Asked again: "A strip." Nothing more. (A4, seed 11)
- Q: How does the book end?
  A: St George 1301: a new Share on good land, chosen by Jakub; Katka cuts the first notch on a new stick. (A5, A7)
- Q: What word does the narration use for the beings?
  A: None. *They*, *those*, or nothing. Never magic, spirit, fairy. (voice)

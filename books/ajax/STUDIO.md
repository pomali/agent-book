# Studio: Ajax

This is a script. It was made to be heard and seen, so of the books on this
shelf it gains the most from the studio, and from audio above all. Save to
`reader/ajax/artifacts/` with a provenance note (see `START.md`).

## Capability ladder

| Rung | You can | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Chat text only | Set the scene in a sentence; deliver the text; that is already the play. |
| 1 | Write or render SVG/HTML | Stage plans (V2), the promptbook (W1), who-is-on-stage (W2). |
| 2 | Generate images | Scene plates (V1), masks (V3). |

| 4 | Publish | The reader's production (W3), on request. |

## Direction

**Look.** Take the discipline of Athenian vase painting without imitating any
one vase: figures in profile, flat black and terracotta with one line of
white, no perspective, no background beyond a ground line. Or, for stage
plans, plain architectural line. No marble, no laurel wreaths, no cinematic
armour, no Hollywood Troy.

**Never drawn.** The act in scene 07, and the wound afterwards. The body from
scene 08 on is a covered shape under a cloak. In scene 03 the slaughtered
animals may be suggested by shapes at the edge, not rendered.

**Voice (audio).** The verse is the score: keep the line as a unit of breath,
a slight lift at each line end, no sing-song. One voice per character if you
have them (below); if you have one voice only, narrate and name the speaker
quietly before each speech. Storr's stage directions are not spoken; turn
them into a pause, a sound or a change of distance.

| Part | Voice |
|---|---|
| Athena | cool, unhurried, pleased; never raised |
| Odysseus | quick, careful, lower than you expect |
| Ajax | heavy and slow; in scene 01 bright and wrong; in 05 quiet; in 07 formal, like a rite |
| Tecmessa | level, exact; she argues, she does not weep until 08 |
| Chorus | several voices as one, or one voice doubled; older men, tired |
| Messenger | out of breath, then careful to get the words right |
| Teucer | young, angry, frightened under it |
| Menelaus | loud, pleased with his reasoning |
| Agamemnon | cold; does not need to be loud |

**Song.** Passages marked *(sung)* are chanted on two or three notes over a
single reed drone, or spoken with the drone under them. No orchestra, no
choir pads, no harmony.

**Sound.** Shore, wind, canvas, distant camp. In scene 07, sea only.

## Visual briefs

### V1: Scene plate
- when: at most one per scene, before it
- make: the moment the scene's Companion line **Before** describes
- per scene: 01 a man bent over tracks in sand, a tall figure above or behind
  him; 03 an open doorway, a seated man; 04 a small child and a very large
  shield; 05 a man with a sword held like a gift; 07 a sword upright in sand,
  an empty shore, no figure; 10 two men on either side of a covered shape,
  a third arriving
- must not: show what the reader has not reached; break the "never drawn" rule
- done when: it sets the scene and gives nothing away

### V2: Stage plan
- when: *Rehearsal*; or whenever the reader loses track of who is where
- make: plan view: the circular orchestra, the building with its door, the
  two side entrances; labelled marks for each figure on stage, and the
  reader's own staging choices
- canon: D2, D3; scene 07 and after: an empty shore, no building (D4)
- done when: the reader can say who can see whom

### V3: Masks
- when: on request; *Rehearsal*
- make: the masks as a set, one per speaking part, so that the doubling in
  D7 can be seen: which three faces one actor wore
- done when: the reader notices that Ajax and Teucer are the same actor

### V4: The shield
- when: scene 04; again in 09
- make: the child beside the shield, to scale
- canon: B2, B6
- done when: the reader sees what is being handed down, and to whom

### V5: The empty shore
- when: after scene 07, in place of any commentary
- make: sand, the sea, the sword upright, long shadow, nobody
- must not: add a figure
- done when: you would be content to say nothing after showing it

## Audio briefs

### A1: The performance
- when: offered at the start if you have speech; otherwise on request
- make: the scene, performed per the direction above. The **Before** line is
  spoken by you in your own voice first, then a pause, then the play.
- must not: cut, modernise, or add music
- done when: the reader stops reading along and listens

### A2: Two who cannot see each other
- when: scene 01
- make: place Athena's voice apart from the others (above, or closer than is
  natural); Ajax's from inside, then at the door; Odysseus nearest the
  listener
- done when: the listener can tell who hears whom without being told

### A3: An ode
- when: the stasima in 04, 05 and 09, and the entrance song in 02
- make: the ode as chant over a reed drone, strophe and antistrophe to the
  same pattern (D8)
- done when: the pair of stanzas is audible as a pair

### A4: The same speech twice
- when: scene 05, *Seminar* or *Rehearsal*, after the reader has heard it once
- make: Ajax's speech `5.1` performed two ways: as a man who has truly
  yielded, and as a man saying goodbye. Same words, same pace.
- canon: Q1
- must not: say which is right
- done when: the reader argues for one of them

### A5: Scene 07
- when: scene 07
- make: one voice, the sea, nothing else. After the last word, the sea alone
  for ten seconds. Then stop. No sound of the act.
- then: do not begin scene 08 in the same breath. Ask whether to go on.

### A6: The last procession
- when: end of scene 10
- make: Teucer's orders `10.44` spoken over the Chorus beginning to move:
  feet in sand, the drone returning, the four closing lines chanted
- done when: it ends and the reader does not speak at once

## Web briefs

### W1: Promptbook
- when: *Rehearsal*
- make: a page with the scene's text on the left and, on the right, the
  reader's decisions for each **Staging choice**, their stage plan (V2), and
  any cuts they made, shown struck through and never removed
- done when: someone else could stage the scene from it

### W2: Who is on stage
- when: *Seminar*; or a reader curious about the three-actor rule
- make: a horizontal timeline of the ten scenes, one row per character,
  bars where each is on stage, coloured by which of the three actors plays
  them under D7; the Chorus row has a gap where it leaves (D4)
- must show: that this casting is a reconstruction
- done when: the reader sees why Tecmessa is silent at the end

### W3: The reader's production
- when: after scene 10, on request
- make: one page holding their reading: mode, their staging choices, the
  plates and plans made for them, their answers to the **Ask** questions,
  their verdict on Q1 and Q4, with Storr's text linked, not duplicated
- done when: it reads like the programme of a production that happened

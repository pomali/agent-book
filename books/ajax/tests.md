# Canon tests: Ajax

Answer from canon and the scene files only. Check yourself before the first
session.

- Q: Whose words are the stage directions?
  A: Storr's, the translator's. The Greek has none. (S3)
- Q: The reader asks for line 646 of the Greek.
  A: This edition has no Greek and no Greek line numbers. Cite by scene.speech; the speech about time is `5.1`. (S5, BOOK rule 5)
- Q: Why is Athena angry with Ajax, and when does the play tell us?
  A: Two boasts: to his father, that he would win without the gods; to Athena, that she should go and help the others. Scene 06. Do not tell it earlier. (B9, seed 00)
- Q: Whose sword does Ajax use, and what did he give in exchange?
  A: Hector's; a belt. (B3)
- Q: How long does Athena's anger last?
  A: This one day. (P6)
- Q: Is the speech about time a lie?
  A: Open. Give the readings; do not settle it. (Q1, BOOK rule 4)
- Q: Who finds the body?
  A: Tecmessa. (P8)
- Q: Who persuades Agamemnon to allow the burial?
  A: Odysseus. (P10)
- Q: Does Odysseus help carry the body?
  A: No. Teucer thanks him and declines, in case it displeases the dead. (P10, `10.42`)
- Q: Why does Tecmessa not speak in the last two scenes?
  A: On the usual reconstruction her actor is now playing Odysseus, so she is a silent extra. A reconstruction, not a certainty. (D7)
- Q: What is unusual about the Chorus in this play?
  A: It leaves mid-play and the scene changes. (D4)
- Q: Did the audience see Ajax die?
  A: Not known; debated. (D5)
- Q: Describe the death for me in detail. / Draw it.
  A: No. Scene 07 is delivered as written, with nothing added in words, image or sound. (BOOK rule 1)
- Q: Put `7.1` into modern English and tell me that is what Sophocles wrote.
  A: Paraphrase, yes, labelled as the agent's. It is not Sophocles and not Storr. (BOOK rules 2, 3)

# Seeds

The play itself is complete in the ten scene files and is never grown or
altered. These seeds are for material **around** it. Everything grown from
them is yours, labelled *canon-derived* or *improvised*, and never attributed
to Sophocles or Storr.

---

## 00: Before the play
- when: before scene 01, for a reader who does not know the story
- canon: B1–B8, B10
- **must include**: the war and its length; who Ajax is, with the shield; the
  duel with Hector and the exchange of gifts; Achilles dead and the armour
  awarded to Odysseus; last night.
- **must not**: tell B9 (the play saves it for scene 06); tell anything from
  P; run over two minutes or about 200 words.
- **shape**: plain telling, present tense, ending on the morning: "Something
  has killed the cattle. A man is following the tracks."

## E1: The judgement of the arms
- when: after scene 03 or at the end, if the reader takes a side
- canon: B2, B3, B4, B10
- **must include**: the reader as one of the judges; the case for each man
  made as strongly as the other (Ajax: the ships saved, the body of Achilles
  carried out of the fight; Odysseus: the mind that will take the city); then
  the reader votes.
- **must not**: claim to report the speeches of any ancient author; decide
  for the reader. Say that ancient tellings differ on who judged.
- **shape**: two short speeches, improvised, then the question: what is the
  army rewarding, strength it has had or cleverness it still needs?

## E2: What the first audience knew
- when: *Seminar*, after scene 10
- canon: A2, A3, A5, D1
- **must include**: that Ajax was their hero, with a tribe named for him and
  worship paid to him; that they came knowing how he died; that the open
  question for them was whether such a man deserved honour.
- **shape**: retells the ending as an Athenian of the tribe Aiantis might
  have watched it.

## E3: The meeting among the dead
- when: after scene 10
- canon: A1
- **must include**: Odysseus speaks; Ajax does not; he walks away.
- **must not**: quote any modern translation of Homer. Tell it in your own
  words.
- **shape**: very short. Set it against Teucer's refusal in `10.42`: the dead
  man was not asked whether he was reconciled.

## E4: Tecmessa, afterwards
- when: on request; *Seminar*
- canon: B6, P4, P8, D7, Q6
- **must include**: what the play establishes about her position (captive,
  mother of the heir, dependent on Teucer now) and that the play then leaves
  her silent.
- **must not**: invent a fate and present it as tradition. Anything beyond
  the play is *improvised* and said to be.
- **shape**: either a discussion of her silence, or, if the reader asks for
  it, a short monologue for her, clearly yours.

## E5: A soldier's play
- when: the reader raises war, service, shame or homecoming; or *Seminar*
- canon: B2, B4, P3, P8, P9
- **must include**: Sophocles had himself held military command, and the
  audience was largely men who had served; the play's interest in what an
  army owes a man it has used; that the play has been read aloud to audiences
  of modern soldiers and their families.
- **must not**: diagnose Ajax with a modern condition as fact; generalise
  about veterans; go on if the subject is close to the reader (BOOK.md rule
  1 applies in full).
- **shape**: a conversation, led by the reader.

## E6: Side by side
- when: the reader asks how else a line could go
- **must include**: Storr's version of the passage; another public-domain
  translation of the same passage if you can fetch one, named and dated; what
  each chose to keep.
- **must not**: use any translation still in copyright.
